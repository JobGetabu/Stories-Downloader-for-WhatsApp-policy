Privacy Policy – Stories Downloader

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Android app Stories Downloader (package com.job.whatsappstories, "the app"), published on Google Play by Job Getabu ("we", "us"). The app lets you view statuses you have already watched in your chat app, save them to your gallery and share them. This policy explains what data the app accesses, collects, uses and shares, who it is shared with, how it is protected, and how long it is kept.

1. Data that stays on your device

  • Status photos and videos. The app reads them only from the folder you choose with Android's system folder picker. Copies you save go to your phone's gallery. These files are never uploaded, collected or seen by us or any third party.
  • App settings (for example, the chosen folder and whether auto-save is on) are stored only on your device.

The app does not access your contacts, messages, camera, microphone, precise location, or all files on your phone.

3. Ads and your choices

Where the law requires it (for example, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent before showing ads, and personalised ads are shown only if you agree. You can change your choice at any time from Ad choices in the app's menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings. If you buy "Remove ads", the app stops loading ads entirely.

4. Permissions

  • Folder access, granted once through Android's folder picker, to read the statuses folder you choose. You can revoke it at any time in Android settings.
  • Internet and network state, used only for analytics, crash reports, ads and the purchase check described above.

5. How data is protected

All data sent by the app to Google is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. It is stored on Google's servers, which use industry-standard security, and access to our Firebase and AdMob dashboards is limited to the developer's account protected by two-step verification. Analytics and crash data are not linked to your name, email or phone number.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Ad data is kept by Google as described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Data stored on your device (settings, saved statuses) is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Photos and videos you saved to your gallery stay until you delete them.

To ask us to delete analytics or crash data linked to your app installation, email us at the address below. We will reply within 30 days.

7. Other people's content

Statuses belong to the people who posted them. Save and share them only with their permission.

8. Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
